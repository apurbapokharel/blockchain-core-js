Unanimous consensus through AI deliberation

Blockchains agree on data. We make them agree on decisions.

Blockchains today settle a question by counting votes or by picking the fastest node, and nobody gives a reason. Here, AI agents on separate blockchain nodes debate a real case in turns. The chain records the decision only when every node holds the same result.
